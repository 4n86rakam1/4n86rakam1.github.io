end of life this November, and rather than shop around for another generator I
wrote my own — I wanted to decide the URLs, the search and the layout myself.

More writeups and notes will land here.

Not quite vibe coding: I read the diffs and pushed back plenty. But describing
what I wanted and arguing with what came back turned out to be a fast way to
build it.
